**0.626 N·m** holding it down. A ball in the up CELL pushes back with `m·g·z`,
where `z` is its horizontal distance from the pivot axis. Measured onset: **12 POLLEN**
or **8 NECTAR**, pooling at a 9–10 in lever arm.
