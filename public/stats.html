What still isn't done — and what's simply not possible

  • FLOWERs score nothing. We proved why: the intake physically cannot push a ball back out — 4 balls in, roller reversed 6 seconds, 4 balls still there, at every flower. But a soft lob into the top of the tube does work (1061 rpm, hood 62°, 12 in away). The behaviour isn't built yet.
  • No shots at full speed in a straight line. At 1.56 m/s you cross the shooting area in under a second and spend most of it 60°+ off the goal's opening. It used to fire there anyway and miss every ball; now it correctly holds.
  • The shooting area is small (13 sq ft) because the hive sits only 54 in from the back wall and the shot table starts at 32 in. That is the field's shape, not a bug.
  • Six physics constants are still guesses — air drag, ball bounce, wheel grip, launch scatter, hive weight. Every number above inherits them, and real-robot measurements would replace them.
  • Not started: the robot model built from ROBOT_BUILD.md, and the rest of the interface work.
